Our story
TWO CITIES.
ONE OBSESSION.

Born from a late-night argument between a Dallas pitmaster and a Bangalore brewmaster, The Fusion Tap was never meant to make sense. That's exactly why it works. Inspired by the grand brewery halls of Marathahalli and the raw energy of Deep Ellum — 800 seats, one neon waterfall, zero compromise.
